# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for practitioners, students, researchers and
developers of every background, language and tradition.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Assume good faith; disagree about ideas, not people.
- Be even-handed about the contested evidence base of homeopathy — critique the data and the
  method, not the person.
- Respect privacy: never post patient information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or sharing of patient-identifiable information.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **sohilmomin2000@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.
