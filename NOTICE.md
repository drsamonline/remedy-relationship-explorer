## Method

Relationships were parsed from each book's *Relations* sections by OCR, resolved against a remedy-name
vocabulary, merged across sources and cross-referenced. The full method is in
[`docs/data.md`](docs/data.md). The keynotes are quoted verbatim, each labelled with its source.
