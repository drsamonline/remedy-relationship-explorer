# Licensing FAQ

**Q. Can I use it for free?**
Yes — for personal study, teaching and reference.

**Q. Can I use it in my clinic?**
Yes, for your own study and reference. If your clinic charges for a service that *incorporates*
the software, that is commercial use and needs a licence.

**Q. Can I modify it?**
Yes, for non-commercial use — keep the attribution and licence notice, and link back. Please
contact the author before redistributing a modified version.

**Q. Can I put it in my own app or product?**
Not without a commercial licence.

**Q. I'm a school / college / university / teaching hospital.**
You can get a free or discounted licence on request — just email.

**Q. Are the books copyrighted?**
No. Boericke (1927), Clarke (1900) and Hering (1879) are all in the public domain. Only the
application code is copyrighted.

**Q. Is it medical advice?**
No. It is a study and reference tool built from OCR'd historical texts. Verify against the
printed sources and consult a qualified practitioner.
